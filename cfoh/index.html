A note from the founder

I built this because my wallet was empty every month.

When I was building my one-person marketing business, the biggest hurdle was not getting clients. It was that it did not matter how much I made. By the end of every month I had nothing left, because spending always quietly caught up with income.

I thought I had a discipline problem. The real problem was that I could not “see” my money. One balance, a dozen subscriptions, a tax bill somewhere in the future, and no idea what I could actually pay myself. So every number looked spendable, and I spent it.

What fixed it was not a spreadsheet. It was a set of habits: give every dollar a job the day it arrives, pay yourself on purpose, set tax aside before you can miss it, and decide on new costs with the numbers in front of you. I built CFoh! to run those habits for me.

It worked for my business, so I am opening it up to anyone who earns well and still wonders where it all went.
