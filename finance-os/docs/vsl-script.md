# CFoh! explainer VSL (2 minutes, music + on-screen text, no voiceover)

Format: silent explainer. Screen recording of the app (demo workspace) with text cards, slow zooms, and an upbeat but calm track underneath. One idea per card. Captions large enough for a phone.

Timing is for ~115 seconds. Each card shows for 3 to 5 seconds unless noted.

## 0:00 to 0:12 · Hook (black background, white text, one line at a time)

1. "You made good money this month."
2. "So why is the account empty again?"
3. Card flips to a bank app balance that shrinks as subscription logos stack up around it.

## 0:12 to 0:30 · The problem (why)

4. "One balance for everything makes every dollar look spendable."
5. Zoom into a long list of tool charges: "Subscriptions stack up quietly."
6. "Pay yourself whatever is left… and hope tax works out."
7. Card: "This is not a discipline problem. It is a visibility problem."

## 0:30 to 0:48 · The idea (what)

8. CFoh! wordmark animates in (CF, then *oh!* slides in italic).
9. "Give every dollar a job the moment it lands."
10. Cut to the dashboard allocation table. Zoom on the six rows: Operating 25%, Owner pay 35%, Tax 15%, Profit 10%, Reserve 10%, Growth 5%.
11. "Six envelopes. Adds up exactly. Your percentages, your rules."

## 0:48 to 1:25 · How it works (product, one feature per beat)

12. Transactions page, record a $4,000 client payment. Dashboard numbers tick up. Text: "Record what you collect."
13. Expense health card, progress bar filling toward the ceiling. Text: "A ceiling on tools. Unspent allowance stays in the business."
14. Drag the expense bar past the ceiling, alert appears. Text: "It tells you the day you overspend, not at month end."
15. Owner & personal page. Text: "Pay yourself on purpose. Household first, then the rest."
16. Reserves page, coverage bar. Text: "Build a cushion before you hire."
17. Trends page, weekly then monthly. Text: "Watch the result line climb."
18. Month-end page, ticking the ten steps, Close button. Text: "Close the month. The report writes itself."
19. Phone mock-up with the home-screen icon, then laptop showing the same dashboard. Text: "Phone and laptop. Same numbers."

## 1:25 to 1:45 · Trust

20. Text: "Private by design." Sub-line: "Add a passphrase and even we cannot read your numbers."
21. Text: "Built by a one-person agency owner who kept ending the month with an empty wallet." Founder photo, small.

## 1:45 to 2:00 · Call to action

22. Wordmark + URL: abdurrub.com/cfoh
23. "Start your free month. No card."
24. Final card, two lines: "Make better financial decisions." / "Track your finances, spending and profit without hiring a CFO."

## Production notes

- Record the app from the demo workspace (homepage → Live demo) at 1440×900, light theme, browser chrome hidden.
- Zoom level 1.0 → 1.12 over each clip; cut on the beat.
- Music: calm, upbeat, no vocals (Epidemic Sound or Artlist, licensed). Duck slightly under text cards.
- Captions: Inter or Geist Bold, white on a dark 55% band, 34 to 40px at 1280 wide.
- Keep claims descriptive (what the app does). No outcome promises, no tax advice.
- A silent, auto-generated version of beats 10 to 18 is produced by `node scripts/make-tour.mjs` as `public/cfoh-demo.mp4`; use it as a rough cut or placeholder.
